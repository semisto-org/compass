---
title: Trainer Checklist
description: Checklist for trainers before, during, and after a session.
---

## One week before

- [ ] Confirm participant list
- [ ] Prepare materials and handouts
- [ ] Check venue / outdoor site conditions
- [ ] Send reminder to participants

## Day of

- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early
- [ ] Set up materials and signage
- [ ] Test any AV equipment
- [ ] Welcome participants, do introductions

## After the session

- [ ] Collect feedback forms
- [ ] Upload photos to shared drive
- [ ] Send follow-up email with resources
- [ ] Log session in the Academy tracker
