## Annual obligations

- [ ] **General Assembly** — Hold at least once per year
- [ ] **Annual accounts** — File with the Greffe du Tribunal
- [ ] **UBO Register** — Update within one month of any change
- [ ] **Tax declaration** — File corporate tax return (even if exempt)

## Key deadlines

| Obligation | Deadline |
|-----------|----------|
| General Assembly | Within 6 months of fiscal year end |
| Annual accounts filing | Within 30 days of GA approval |
| UBO Register update | Ongoing (within 1 month of changes) |
| Tax return | As per notification from SPF Finances |
