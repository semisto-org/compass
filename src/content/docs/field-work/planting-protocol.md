---
title: Planting Protocol
description: Standard procedure for planting in Semisto forest gardens.
---

## Before planting

1. Check the planting plan in Terranova
2. Verify species availability in the nursery
3. Prepare tools: spade, mulch, stakes, labels

## Planting steps

1. **Dig the hole** — 2× the root ball width, same depth
2. **Place the plant** — Crown at soil level
3. **Backfill** — Mix native soil with compost
4. **Water** — Generous initial watering
5. **Mulch** — 10cm layer around the base, not touching the stem
6. **Label** — Attach species tag with planting date

## After planting

- Log the planting in Terranova
- Schedule first monitoring check (2 weeks)
- Take a photo for records
