## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| **Teal** | `#0D9488` | Primary accent, links, CTAs |
| **Cream** | `#faf8f5` | Backgrounds |
| **Warm grey** | `#5c5c5c` | Body text |
| **Dark** | `#1a1a1a` | Headings |
| **Forest green** | `#065f46` | Secondary accent |

## Tone of voice

- **Warm** — We're approachable and human
- **Clear** — No jargon, no fluff
- **Grounded** — Rooted in practice, not theory
- **Hopeful** — We believe in what we do

## Logo usage

- Always use the official logo files from the shared drive
- Minimum clear space: half the logo height on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolour the logo
